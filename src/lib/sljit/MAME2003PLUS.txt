sljit for mame2003-plus
=======================

Source: https://github.com/zherczeg/sljit, commit 39c508d (2026-09-30),
sljit_src folder only, unmodified. BSD 2-clause (LICENSE).

Used by the optional recompilers: MIPS III (src/cpu/mips/mips3jit.c) and
PSX CPU (src/cpu/mips/psxjit.c). Added here:

sljit_mame.h   configuration shared by the recompilers, and
               SLJIT_MAME_SUPPORTED: the hosts they support (little-endian
               x86 and ARM, 32 and 64-bit, not where run-time code
               generation is forbidden or restricted)
sljit_mame.c   builds sljit once (only on those hosts; elsewhere nothing
               of it is compiled)
